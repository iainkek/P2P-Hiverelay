1 Connect & configure

Demo mode simulates the full lifecycle locally. Switch to Live and point at a deployed escrow to use real USD₮.
New here? On Base Sepolia: deploy your own test escrow (mints 1,000 USD₮), or grab test USD₮ from the faucet to play with an existing one.

2 Deposit bankroll

Approve (if needed) and deposit USD₮ into the table escrow. You can top up any time before the session settles.

3 Settle & cash out

When the session settles, your net is recorded on-chain. Pull it with Withdraw.

① Agreed result

The session's final balances (deposit ± net), as everyone agreed. In production these come from the reducer; paste or type them here.

② Sign your share

Sign the agreed result with your wallet (EIP-191). Share your signature with the table — every seat must sign.

③ Submit on-chain

Once you've collected every seat's signature, anyone submits them to settle the net on-chain.
